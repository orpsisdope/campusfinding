About the project

CampusFind Team

CampusFind is a simple lost and found system for university students. It connects a browser frontend, an Express backend and a PostgreSQL database in one fullstack application.

Purpose

Students often lose small personal belongings around campus. CampusFind gives them one place to publish lost and found reports, read existing reports and mark a report as resolved after an item has been returned.
